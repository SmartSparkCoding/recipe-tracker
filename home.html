Cookies! (School Recipe)

8 - 10 Cookies | Created 16 April 2026


Ingredients: Butter, Sugar, Brown Sugar, 1/2 Egg, Vanilla Extract, Salt, Self-Raising Folour and Chocolate Chips.

Instructions: Preheat the oven to Gas Mark 4. Cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Beat the eff and add half to the mixture. Add the vanilla extract. Sieve in the flour and mix. Add the brown sugar and mix it in well. Stir in the choc chips until the dough forms. Roll into gold ball sized portions. Place on a lined baking tray, leaving space between each cookie. Bake for 10 to 15 minutes until golden! Then, let them cool for 10 minutes, and eat them all in one go!

Generic Flapjack Recipe

Makes 8 to 12 Flapjacks | Created 16 April 2026


Ingredients: Rolled oats, sugar, margarine and golden syrup.

Instructions: Preheat the oven to Gas Mark 5. Line a square tin with baking paper. Place the margine and golden syrup in a saucepan and melt over low heat. In a large bowl, mix the oats and sugar, then pour the melted mixture into the oats and mix well. Press the mixture into the tin and flatten with a spoon. Bake for 20 minutes, wait 5 minutes to cool and cut into squares. ENJOY!!!!
